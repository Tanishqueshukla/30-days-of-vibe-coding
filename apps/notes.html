Why I'm Doing This

I'm a student who can write code, but not build apps — and I think that's exactly the point. The tools for building software have fundamentally changed. AI can now write, debug, and deploy code. I wanted to prove that someone with zero programming experience can ship real, functional applications.

This challenge is simple: 30 days, 30 apps, zero lines of hand-written code. Every single application is built entirely through AI prompts — crafted conversations with tools like Claude and ChatGPT. I describe what I want, iterate on the result, and ship it.

I'm documenting every step in public. The wins, the fails, the weird bugs, and the breakthroughs. Because the future of building isn't about syntax — it's about ideas, taste, and knowing what to ask for.
